Q1.What are the different types of expressions? 
Ans:Infix, Prefix, Postfix

Q2.Give examples of all types of expressions. 
Ans: Infix: a+b
Prefix: +ab Postfix: ab+

Q3.Which data structure is used for evaluation of expression?
Ans: Stack

Q4.Convert infix expression a+b+c+d to prefix expression.
Ans:+++abcd

Q5.Convert infix expression a+b+c+d to postfix expression. 
Ans: ab+c+d+
